## Reading

The reading assignment is @langton1993.

There is *a lot* written on this topic in the wake of Langton's paper. @bauer2015 is one example. [Section 4.1](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/pornography-censorship/#FemiArguAgaiPorn) of @west2022 offers an overview. @eaton2017 leans further into aesthetics rather than philosophy of language, offering an account of feminist pornography that is grounded in views about the power and authority of pornography that are similar to those found in Langton. (Those of you who recently attended the Illinois Philosophical Association may have met Eaton!)
