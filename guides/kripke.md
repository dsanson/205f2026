The reading assignment is selections from the first two lectures in @kripke1980a:

-  [Lecture I](https://canvas.illinoisstate.edu/courses/53417/files/8685475/download?download_frd=1)
-  [Lecture II](https://canvas.illinoisstate.edu/courses/53417/files/8685470/download?download_frd=1)

These links are to files hosted on Canvas.

More specifically, I want you to read all of Lecture I, and, for Lecture II, up to page 97. This is a lot. But this is also a different kind of reading: it is transcriptions from a lecture, so it is a bit more chatty than what we have been reading. And it was published as a trade paperback, so the pages themselves are a bit smaller. Still, it is a lot.

I recommend that *before* you read Lecture I, you take a look at the first page of Lecture II. There Kripke lays out six theses and a condition, which, he says, give you the "Frege-Russell" theory of proper names, or a generalization of it. None of that is going to make much sense to you yet, but it provides a very good synopsis of a lot of what happens in the first lecture.
